# CA1_Data_Exploration_and_Preparation

## Project Title

Exploring Large Energy Users' Electricity Consumption in Ireland

## Domain

Energy

## Dataset

MEC04 - Large Energy Users Metered Electricity Consumption

Source: Central Statistics Office (CSO), Ireland.

## Problem Statement

Electricity consumption by large energy users is an important part of Ireland's overall energy demand. Analysing how electricity consumption varies over time and across different consumption categories can help identify patterns, variations and unusual observations. This project will explore and prepare the MEC04 dataset to investigate electricity consumption patterns among large energy users in Ireland.

## Aim

The aim of this project is to explore and prepare a real-world dataset on metered electricity consumption by large energy users in Ireland, identify meaningful patterns and relationships in the data, and apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to investigate the main dimensions of variation within the dataset.

## Research Questions

1. How does electricity consumption among large energy users vary over time?

2. How does electricity consumption vary across different days and hourly consumption periods?

3. What unusual patterns or variations can be identified in electricity consumption among large energy users?

4. Which features contribute most to the variation observed in the electricity consumption data?

## Project Objectives

- Inspect and prepare the selected real-world electricity consumption dataset.
- Identify and handle missing values, data-quality issues and potential outliers.
- Examine the structure, attributes and distributions of the dataset.
- Explore variations and relationships between relevant features.
- Identify unusual patterns using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Apply an appropriate categorical encoding technique.
- Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for dimensionality reduction.
- Interpret the PCA results and identify the features contributing most to the observed variation.
- Present the key findings through a PowerPoint poster and a short video presentation.
